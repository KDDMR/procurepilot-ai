# Example Procurement Policy

This fictional policy exists only for the ProcurePilot AI portfolio demo.

## Approval thresholds

Purchases below EUR 5,000 may be approved automatically after a successful policy check.
Purchases from EUR 5,000 require approval from the requester's line manager. Purchases from
EUR 25,000 require an additional Procurement review. Purchases of EUR 100,000 or more require
line-manager, Procurement and Executive Board approval. Splitting purchases to avoid an
approval threshold is prohibited.

## Competitive sourcing

Purchases of EUR 10,000 or more should compare at least three suitable suppliers. A single-source
purchase above this threshold requires a written business justification and Procurement approval.
Urgency alone is not sufficient justification and never bypasses an approval control.

## Data protection and security

Services processing restricted data require Security and Data Protection review before purchase.
Services processing confidential personal data also require Data Protection review. Contracts must
define the processing purpose, retention period, deletion process, access controls, incident handling
and the permitted processing locations. Production credentials must never be included in a request.

## Cloud and AI services

Cloud and AI services must document their data flows, model or service provider, storage location,
access model and operational owner. Restricted business data must not be used for model training.
The requesting team must confirm that generated output remains subject to human review where it
can affect employees, customers, payments, legal obligations or production operations.

## Auditability

Every decision must retain the original request, the applicable policy evidence, identified risk
flags, the chosen approval route and any human decision. Automated recommendations are decision
support only. Human reviewers remain accountable for approvals and rejections.
